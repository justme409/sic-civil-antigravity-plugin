# Civil (SI Civil)

The Civil MCP tools read the SI Civil projects the signed-in person is appointed to: civil construction work
such as roads, kerbs, drainage, pavements and streetscapes.

- Start with `my_projects` to see the projects and the person's role on each.
- `project_status`, `whats_waiting`, `programme_position`, `lot_status` and `list_risks` answer where a project stands.
- `find_documents` lists documents (ITPs, method statements, SWMS, plans, drawings) and `read_document` reads one in full.
- `hold_witness_schedule` gives the hold and witness points of an ITP, with who releases each.
- `search_project` searches everything on the project.
- `add_comment` drafts a comment for the team on a document, lot or ITP item.

Name the document, ITP item, lot or milestone each answer comes from. What a person sees follows their role.
